Step 4 of 6 · Mom & rent

Mom & the pool.

Mom paid R60k for the pool. She's charging ~R5k/m interest on it. Plan: pay her now from revolving credit — that stops the R5k/m interest and lets her resume paying R16k/m rent immediately.

Net effect: avoids ~R30k of mom-interest + R75k of rent flowing 6 months earlier, vs ~R15k extra revolving interest. Net win ≈ +R90k.

Step 5 of 6 · Daily check-in

When for the daily check-in?

30 seconds a day. Pick a time that fits your routine — first coffee, lunch, end of work.

Real push notifications need the app to be open — that's an iOS limitation. The habit of opening the app at the same time each day is what really sticks.
